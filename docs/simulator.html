Shade Engine Simulator

geometry · policy · actuator, one day at a time

Paste a shade_engine zone configuration, set a day and a sky, and watch what the engine would do: the calculator's glare position, the policy ladder switching modes, and the actuator's actual cover commands — deadband, rate limiting, and manual-move holds included. Times are shown in this browser's timezone; illuminance is a synthetic outdoor-sensor model.

Scenario

Policy ladder

The ladder sets each zone's mode: night → open → glare → open → night. Glare releases when bright ends above the elevation gate, on dim, or 5 min before sunset. Zones without a matching mode name keep their current mode.

Manual move

A position that differs from the last command by more than the deadband, outside the settle window, starts a visible hold for the zone's hold_duration.

Zones (configuration.yaml)

Same schema as the integration: window geometry with protect_depth or eye_zone + reflectors, modes (fixed position, glare, or min/max clamp), and motion tuning.
